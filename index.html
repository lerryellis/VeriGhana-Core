Under the hood

How VeriGhana Works

Three layers of AI verification, updated every six hours.

01
Continuous Source Monitoring
Our scraper crawls 60+ Ghanaian government portals and media outlets every 6 hours via GitHub Actions, storing articles in a Supabase database.
02
AI Semantic Embedding
Each article is converted into a 3072-dimension vector using Gemini's embedding model, enabling semantic search that goes beyond simple keyword matching.
03
Cascading Verification
Your claim is matched against indexed content at multiple similarity thresholds. Gemini AI reads the top matches and returns a scored verdict with source citations.
